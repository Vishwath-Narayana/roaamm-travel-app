# Photo credits

Every photo on the ROAAMM landing page is a free stock photo, used under the
[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial and
non-commercial use, no permission needed. Attribution is not required, but we
give it anyway.

The people in these photos are not ROAAMM users. They are seen from behind or
far away, and no photo is presented as a named member.

Files are generated by `node scripts/optimise-images.mjs` from originals kept
in the git-ignored folder `design/stock-originals/<slot>.jpg`. To rebuild, download
each source below into that folder under its slot name, then run the script.
Each slot is cropped (heroes 16:9, cards 4:5, stories 4:3) and written as
`<slot>-<width>.{avif,webp,jpg}`.
